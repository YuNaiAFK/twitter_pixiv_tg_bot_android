Do not run 'pkg upgrade' in the Termux bootstrap, and never let dpkg prompt

The first version ran 'pkg upgrade' and it hung the entire install. This device had 74 packages
to upgrade, and dpkg asks about configuration-file conflicts even under
DEBIAN_FRONTEND=noninteractive - with no terminal to answer it waits forever. Measured from
the PC: six minutes with the prefix stuck at 81 MB, zero .deb files in the apt cache, and apt
sleeping in do_select on an established but idle connection to the mirror. A screenshot of the
phone showed it stopped at "Building dependency tree..." with a blinking cursor.

'pkg upgrade' is also unnecessary: 'pkg install' resolves what it needs.

Also added:

  - dpkg --force-confold/--force-confdef/--use-pty, which suppress the prompts that
    DEBIAN_FRONTEND does not
  - lock cleanup and 'dpkg --configure -a' at the start, so a rerun after an interrupted
    install recovers instead of refusing to proceed
